THE OTHER DUO

Apple made a Duo.
So did we.
Ours is free.

In September 2026 Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo: a book-style foldable with a 7.6-inch inner display, a titanium hinge with more than a hundred parts, and a nano-texture layer so you can feel the crease but not see it. Close it and the inner screen fades while your apps hop to the outer display. Lovely.

We would like to point out that your MacBook has had a hinge, a lid and a fade-to-black this whole time, and that only one of these Duos does something interesting on the way down.

SMALL APP. TANGIBLE MOVEMENT.

Move the lid.
Feel the difference.

01

It follows your hand.

The effect tracks the lid angle in real time. Change your mind halfway and the desktop comes back with you. No commitment required.

02

Hold still. See clearly.

Park the lid at any comfortable angle. After 1–5 seconds of your choosing the desktop clears, so you can keep working at 70° like a person with a plan.

03

Quiet when you are.

Settled previews stop rendering, blur work is cached, and the refresh rate bows to power and temperature. Your fans stay off. Your battery is unbothered.

04

Your screen stays yours.

Frames stay in memory on your Mac. No uploads, recordings, accounts or analytics. We could not tell you what is on your desktop if you asked.

TWELVE WAYS TO CLOSE

Pick a feel.

Duo is the default. The iPhone Duo has one closing animation. We have twelve, and none of them is a fade. Fine: Ghost is a fade. A tasteful one.

01 / DUO · DEFAULT

Your desktop.
Following the fold.

The desktop swells around the hinge as the lid closes.

02 / GHOST

The lid moves.
The desktop stays behind.

The desktop holds its resting plane as the lid tilts and gently falls out of focus. The fade, done properly.

03 / ROLL

A flexible sheet.
A rolling hinge.

The desktop curls into a roll that travels down to the hinge.

04 / SHUTTER

Rigid panels.
One smooth close.

Rigid panels telescope behind each other into the hinge.

05 / FLEX

A little tension.
A gentle bend.

One bowing flexible display collapses toward the hinge.

06 / IRIS

Eight blades.
A closing aperture.

Eight overlapping blades close an aperture above the hinge.

07 / FOLD

A crease in the middle.
A half folding over.

The display creases across the middle and the top half folds down over the bottom. The crease Apple spent years hiding, back by popular demand.

08 / ACCORDION

Pleats gather.
Like a paper fan.

Pleats zig-zag and gather toward the hinge like a paper fan.

09 / LOUVER

Slats tilt.
Blinds close.

Horizontal slats tilt and overlap like closing window blinds.

10 / CARD

One rigid card.
Tipping back.

The whole desktop tips back as one rigid card in perspective.

11 / CURTAIN

Drapes draw.
Settle at the hinge.

Drapes draw together from both sides and settle at the hinge.

12 / BLACKHOLE

A horizon opens.
The desktop falls in.

The desktop swirls into a black hole opening at the hinge. Nothing escapes, including your unsaved tabs. (They are fine. It is a shader.)

COMPATIBILITY · MACBOOK ONLY

It needs a lid.
A specific kind of lid.

Macbook Duo reads the continuous lid-angle sensor Apple builds into recent MacBooks. No sensor, no angle; no angle, no show. The app checks at launch and tells you straight away, and Replay still shows every effect on the built-in preview so you can window-shop.

SUPPORTED

The ones with the sensor.

MacBook Air with M2 or newer. 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro with M1 Pro, M1 Max or newer. macOS 13 Ventura or later.

INTEL PREVIEW

Probably. Tell us.

2019 16-inch MacBook Pro. A native Intel build ships with every release; physical verification on Intel is still pending, so reports are welcome.

UNSUPPORTED

Lid, yes. Sensor, no.

M1 MacBook Air, 13-inch MacBook Pro with M1 or M2, and Intel models that only report open or closed. They know the lid moved; they refuse to say how far.

ALSO NO, SINCE YOU ASKED

Things without the right lid.

iMac (no lid). Mac mini (no lid). Mac Studio (no lid, many ports). iPad (a cover is not a lid). iPhone Duo (has a hinge, wrong hinge). Your fridge (has a hinge, no macOS).

External displays are not animated. The desktop on your MacBook's own screen is the star; everything else is the audience.

A MINUTE TO GET STARTED

Download. Drag. Open.

No pre-order. No launch day queue. No 5:00 a.m. Pacific.

  1. Download and move.

    Open Macbook-Duo.dmg for Apple silicon or the Intel preview, then drag Macbook Duo into Applications.

  2. Allow the first launch.

    Try opening Macbook Duo. This release is not notarized, so macOS may show "cannot be opened" or "Apple could not verify". Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security → Open Anyway for Macbook Duo, then confirm Open. Apple's guide ↗

  3. Let it follow your lid.

    Press Replay to see the effect without any permission. Then click Enable Macbook Duo and allow Screen Recording when prompted. Reopen if macOS asks. Frames stay in memory on your Mac.

Macbook Duo speaks English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese. Opt in to Open at login (launches start paused) and hide the menu-bar icon if you like. Use Check for Updates… → Install & Relaunch for architecture-aware updates from the official GitHub release; checks run only when you ask, and no desktop frames leave your Mac. If macOS blocks an update, use Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, then retry in the recovery dialog or restore the previous app. Prefer the Mac App Store?
